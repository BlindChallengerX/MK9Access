Mortal Kombat 9 (2011) – Bread and Butter Combos

A screen-reader-friendly reference for every character in Mortal Kombat Komplete Edition: the most useful kombo strings and what each one is for, practical "bread and butter" (BnB) combos from several different starters, and a short how-to-play summary. 31 characters are covered: the 27 base roster plus the four DLC fighters (Skarlet, Kenshi, Rain, Freddy Krueger).

Each character has three parts: Useful strings, Combos, and How to play. String names and inputs match the game's own move list. Damage percentages are approximate and come from community combo lists; exact values vary with spacing, hit-confirms and game patch, and a number is left out when no source gave one. Combos are listed one per line, each labelled with its starter. Where a combo needs the corner or super meter, it says so.

Notation & legend

MK9 uses four attack buttons. This guide uses the standard numeric notation with directions and spelled-out special-move names.

1 = Front Punch
The quick/lead punch.
2 = Back Punch
The strong/rear punch.
3 = Front Kick
The quick/lead kick.
4 = Back Kick
The strong/rear kick.
Directions: Forward, Back, Up, Down
Forward means toward the opponent, Back means away. "Back+2" means hold Back and press Back Punch. "Forward+3" means Forward Front Kick.
Special move inputs
Written the way the game's move list shows them: directions tapped one after another, then the button. Example: "Down, Back, 3" means tap Down, tap Back, press Front Kick. Each character's Combos section lists the inputs for the specials it uses.
Motions (a few community posts)
QCF = quarter-circle forward (Down, then Down-Forward, then Forward). QCB = quarter-circle back (Down, Down-Back, Back). This page writes special inputs in full instead.
xx
Cancel: cancel the string directly into the special move that follows.
EX (enhanced)
Enhanced special move: the special's input plus the Block button. Costs 1 bar of super meter. This page gives the enhanced move's in-game name with "(EX)" after it, for example "Flame Spear (EX Spear)".
X-Ray
The character's X-Ray super move. Costs all 3 bars of super meter.
jump-in Punch (a.k.a. ji1 / JP / JPS)
Jump toward the opponent and press a Punch to start the combo. "Jump Kick" (JK) is the same with a kick.
NJP
Neutral Jump Punch: jump straight up and press Punch (used to re-launch a juggled opponent).
dash
Quickly dash forward (Forward, Forward) to stay in range for the next hit.
OTG
Off The Ground: a move that hits the opponent while they are lying downed.
Air Throw
A throw performed while both players are airborne.
Uppercut
Down + Back Punch (Down+2), the universal launching uppercut.
,
Separates inputs done in sequence. In a string name such as "Torment (1, 1, 1)" the commas are the hits of the string.
Hit levels: high, mid, low, overhead
High: hits standing opponents and misses many crouching ones. Mid: block standing or crouching. Low: must be blocked crouching. Overhead: must be blocked standing.
Safe / unsafe on block
Safe means the opponent cannot punish you after blocking it. Unsafe or punishable means they can.
Frames
Start-up speed. 60 frames is one second; a lower number is faster. Most jabs are 8 to 12 frames.
Starts combos
A label on strings that lead into a full combo on hit.
Juggle starter / launcher / pop-up
A hit that knocks the opponent into the air so you can keep hitting them.

Button mapping reminder: on a pad, Front Punch / Back Punch / Front Kick / Back Kick are the four face buttons; the Block button is separate and is used (with a punch or kick) for Enhanced and X-Ray inputs.

Johnny Cage

Useful strings

  • Widescreen (2, 1, Forward+2): his main string. First two hits high, last hit an overhead that pops the opponent up. 2 is fast (9 frames) and the string is fairly safe on block. Starts combos
  • Director's Cut (2, 1, Back+4): same start, but the last hit is low. Use it when they block the 2, 1, Forward+2. Nothing combos after it.
  • Take Two (Forward+3, 2): both hits high. Leaves Johnny ahead on hit and on block, so you can repeat it as a frame trap; the opponent's only clean way out is to jump.
  • That's The Ticket (Forward+3, 3, Back+3): fast poke; Forward+3, 3 hits high and cancels into Nut Punch or Flipkick. The last hit, Back+3, is an overhead. Starts combos
  • Money Shot (4, 4): high, safe on block. Used in the middle of juggles before a special. On hit you get a safe jump-in or a free throw attempt.
  • Cross Cutting (1, 3, 2): first hit high, last two mid; leaves Johnny ahead on hit.
  • Pop Up (Back+3): 11 frames, pops the opponent straight up. His quick launcher. Starts combos
  • Axe Kick (Forward+4): overhead that pops up. Slow (28 frames), so use it as a mix-up or after a jump-in. Johnny is ahead if it is blocked. Starts combos

Combos

Specials used: Flipkick (Down, Back, 3), Shadow Kick (Back, Forward, 4), Nut Punch (Back, Down, 1). Enhanced: Ultra Flipkick, Nutcracker.

  • Fast string: 2, 1, Forward+2, 4, 4 xx Flipkick (~30%)
  • Frame trap: Forward+3, 3 xx Nut Punch (~21%; ~25% with Nutcracker, EX Nut Punch)
  • Pop-up: Back+3, dash, 2, 1, Forward+2, dash, 4, 4 xx Flipkick (~31%; ~35% with Ultra Flipkick, EX Flipkick)
  • Pop-up, keeps them standing: Back+3, dash, 1, 1, dash, 4, 4 xx Nut Punch (~27%; Nut Punch leaves them standing, so no wake-up attack)
  • Overhead: Forward+4, dash, 2, 1, Forward+2, dash, 4, 4 xx Flipkick (~34%)
  • Overhead, long: Forward+4, dash, Back+3, dash, 2, 1, Forward+2, dash, 4, 4 xx Flipkick (or Shadow Kick) (~38%)
  • Jump-in: jump-in Punch, Forward+4, dash, 2, 1, Forward+2, Forward+3, Shadow Kick (~32%)
  • Quick punish: Forward+4, Back+3 xx Shadow Kick (~21–25%)
  • X-Ray (parry): X-Ray, dash, Back+3, dash, 2, 1, Forward+2, dash, 4, 4 xx Flipkick (~52%, 3 bars; his X-Ray is a parry, so it starts this when it catches an attack)

How to play

Johnny Cage is a rushdown / pressure character. Fast strings (2, 1, Forward+2) and his Nut Punch keep opponents pinned up close; Shadow Kick and Flipkick are strong combo enders and whiff punishers, Flipkick is one of the best anti-airs in the game, and the Forceballs control space from range. Get in, stagger strings, and bully with the Forward+3, 2 frame trap. Ending combos with Nut Punch leaves the opponent standing, so they can't use a wake-up attack.

Sonya Blade

Useful strings

  • Drop Zone (2, 1, Back+3): 2, 1 cancels into Kartwheel for her standard juggle. Starts combos
  • Mess Hall (Back+2, 1, Forward+2): Back+2 launches and is a strong punisher (17 frames); the string ends in a pop-up you can juggle from. Starts combos
  • Fierce Assault (1, 1, 4): starts with her 7-frame jab, so it punishes moves with very short recovery. 1, 1, 4 cancels into Kartwheel. Starts combos
  • Power Rush (Forward+2, 2, 4): Forward+2, 2 cancels into Kartwheel, or into Military Stance for a mix-up (Lift Off launcher, Tackle, or the Hit The Deck sweep). Starts combos
  • Play Time (3, 1, 2): her follow-up after a jump-in Air Drop; 3, 1 cancels into Leg Grab or Kartwheel. Starts combos
  • Heel Bash (Forward+4): overhead with a lot of hit stun. Use it to stop crouch-blocking.

Combos

Specials used: Kartwheel (Down, Forward, 3), Leg Grab (Back, Forward, 4), Kiss (Down, Back, 1), Military Stance (Down, Back, 2; then Back+2 for Lift Off, or 1+3, 1+2 for Tackle), Air Drop (Down+4 in the air). Enhanced: Krazy Legs (EX Leg Grab).

  • Fast string: 2, 1 xx Kartwheel, dash, Back+2, 1, Forward+2, Jump Kick xx Air Throw (or Air Drop) (~31%)
  • Fast string, X-Ray: 2, 1 xx Kartwheel, dash, Back+2, 1, Forward+2, 2, 1 xx X-Ray (~37%, 3 bars)
  • Fast jab punish: 1, 1, 4 xx Kartwheel, dash, 4 xx Leg Grab (~23%; ~28% with Krazy Legs)
  • Launcher: jump-in Punch, Back+2, 1, Forward+2, Military Stance, Lift Off (Back+2), Uppercut (~30%)
  • Military Stance string: Forward+2, 2 xx Kartwheel, Forward+2, 2 xx Leg Grab (~29%; ~33% with Krazy Legs)
  • Jump-in Air Drop: jump-in Punch, Air Drop, 3, 1, 2 xx Krazy Legs (EX Leg Grab) (~39%, 1 bar; ~34% with plain Leg Grab)
  • Jump-in Air Drop, X-Ray: jump-in Punch, Air Drop, 3, 1 xx X-Ray (~41%, 3 bars)
  • Corner: Air Drop, 4 xx Kartwheel, Back+2, 1 xx Kiss, jump, Air Drop, 3, 1 xx Military Stance, Tackle (1+3, 1+2) (~43%, corner)

How to play

Sonya is a rushdown / footsie hybrid with strong normals and a fast 2, 1 that cancels into Kartwheel for juggles. Energy Rings zone and recover fast, Arc Kick closes distance, Leg Grab ends combos, and Military Stance adds a three-way mix-up after Forward+2, 2. Her Down+4 has one of the longest reaches in the game. Control space with rings, then convert pokes and Air Drops into Kartwheel combos.

Sources: MortalKombatOnline "Kombo Workshop" combo list; GameFAQs community Mortal Kombat Character Guide (2011); swiftninja's Midscreen Combo FAQ (GameFAQs).

Sub-Zero

Useful strings

  • Winter Blade (2, 2, 2): base your offense on 2, 2: fast (2 is 9 frames), safe on block, and it cancels into specials. The last hit is an overhead with a very large hitbox. Starts combos
  • Ice Pick (Back+1, 2, 1): Back+1, 2 is an overhead and safe on block, but slower (Back+1 is 12 frames). Back+1, 2 cancels into Iceball. Starts combos
  • Frost Bitten (2, 1, 2): use as a punisher; cancels into Iceball. Starts combos
  • Ices Up (2, 1, 4): his juggle string after a freeze, ending in Slide or X-Ray.
  • Big Leg (Forward+4): solid poke that hits overhead and cancels into Iceball or Slide. Starts combos
  • 2 xx Iceball: his anti-air of choice against jump-ins.

Combos

Specials used: Iceball (Down, Forward, 3), Slide (Back, Forward, 4), Ice Clone (Down, Back, 1). Enhanced: Power Slide (EX Slide).

  • Fast string: 2, 2 xx Iceball, jump-in Punch, 2, 1, 4 xx Slide (~30%; ~32% with Power Slide)
  • Overhead: jump-in Punch, Back+1, 2 xx Iceball, jump-in Punch, Back+1, 2, 4 (~31%)
  • Overhead, long: Back+1, 2 xx Iceball, NJP, dash, Back+1, 2, then 2, 1, 2 xx Slide (~42%)
  • Overhead poke: Forward+4 xx Iceball, jump-in Punch, 2, 1, 4 xx Slide (~31%; ~33% with Power Slide)
  • Jump-in, 1 bar: jump-in Punch, Back+1, 2 xx Iceball, jump-in Punch, 2, 1, 4 xx Power Slide (EX Slide) (~39%, 1 bar; ~36% with plain Slide)
  • X-Ray: Back+1, 2 xx Iceball, jump-in Punch, Back+1, 2 xx X-Ray (~48%, 3 bars)
  • X-Ray from a poke: Forward+4 xx Iceball, jump-in Punch, 2, 1, 4 xx X-Ray (hold the X-Ray about half a second) (~48%, 3 bars)
  • Corner: 2, 1, 4 xx Iceball, 2, 1, 4 xx Slide (~36%, corner)

How to play

Sub-Zero is a defensive zoner / trap character. Iceball freezes for a full combo from range, the Ice Clone walls off approaches and controls space, and Slide punishes from range (it is very unsafe if blocked). Play patient, place clones deliberately, bait whiffs, and cash every freeze into big damage. Slide is his only wake-up attack, so mix it with back-rolls and Ice Clone.

Sources: MortalKombatOnline "Kombo Workshop" combo list; GameFAQs community Mortal Kombat Character Guide (2011); swiftninja's Midscreen Combo FAQ (GameFAQs).

Scorpion

Useful strings

  • Torment (1, 1, 1): all hits high, safe on block, and 1, 1 cancels into Spear. One of his best strings. Starts combos
  • Damnation (1, 1, 4): all hits high, safe on block, knocks down and pushes them away.
  • Dead End (3, 3, 4): all hits high, safe on block. 3 is his fastest starter (10 frames); 3, 3 cancels into Spear. Starts combos
  • Brimstone (2, 1, 2): first two hits high, last hit mid, safe on block, knockdown. 2, 1 cancels into Spear. Starts combos
  • Gravedigger (2, 1+2): the second hit is a mid launcher, unsafe on block. Follow with Teleport. Starts combos
  • Doom Blade (Forward+2, 1, 4): all hits high, safe on block. Forward+2, 1 cancels into Spear or Teleport and is his usual juggle filler. Starts combos
  • Grievance (Forward+4, 3): first hit low, second high, unsafe on block. Forward+4 cancels into Spear, so it is his low combo starter. Starts combos
  • Blade Overhead (Back+2): overhead launcher; also his best punish for a whiffed teleport. Starts combos

Combos

Specials used: Spear (Back, Back, 1), Teleport (Down, Back, 3; also in the air), Demon Fire (Down, Back, 2), Takedown (Down, Back, 4), Air Throw (throw while airborne). Enhanced: Flame Spear (EX Spear).

  • Anti-air: Jump Kick xx Air Throw (~20%, short and easy anti-air / punish)
  • Fast string: jump-in Punch, 1, 1 xx Spear, NJP, dash, Back+2, dash, 1, 1 xx Teleport, Jump Kick xx Air Throw (~40%)
  • Fastest starter: 3, 3 xx Spear, NJP, dash, Back+2, Jump Kick xx Teleport, Jump Kick xx Air Throw (~36%)
  • Overhead: Back+2, Teleport, Spear, Forward+2, 1, 4 (~21%)
  • Launcher: jump-in Punch, 2, 1+2 xx Teleport, 1, 1 xx Spear, Forward+2, 1 xx Teleport (~33%)
  • Low, X-Ray: Forward+4 xx Spear, NJP, dash, Back+2, Jump Kick xx Teleport, dash, 2, 1 xx X-Ray (~44%, 3 bars)
  • Short X-Ray: 1, 1 xx Spear, Forward+2, 1 xx X-Ray (~48%, 3 bars)
  • Fastest starter, X-Ray: 3, 3 xx Spear, NJP, dash, Back+2, Jump Kick xx Teleport, dash, 2, 1 xx X-Ray (~45%, 3 bars)

After an Air Throw, Hell Fire (EX Demon Fire) is guaranteed. A combo that ends in plain Teleport leaves the opponent staggered, so you can go for another jump-in mix-up instead.

How to play

Scorpion is an aggressive rushdown character built around his Spear and the Teleport "vortex." Spear and Flame Spear start combos from range (only throw a plain Spear inside a combo; Flame Spear has armor). Ending a juggle in Teleport while the opponent is standing forces a guessing game on their landing. Back+2 (overhead) and Forward+4 (low) give him a real high/low mix-up, and Demon Fire is unblockable on the ground.

Sources: MortalKombatOnline "Kombo Workshop" combo list; GameFAQs community Mortal Kombat Character Guide (2011); ELC1837's Scorpion Guide (GameFAQs); swiftninja's Midscreen Combo FAQ (GameFAQs).

Cyrax

Useful strings

  • See Saw (1, 1, 1): safe on block and ends in an overhead. 1 is 9 frames; 1, 1 is one of his preferred safe starters. Starts combos
  • Steel Fists (1, 2, 1): safe on block. Cancels into Ragdoll, or into a Medium Bomb followed by a Net for pressure. Starts combos
  • Mustard (2, 1, 2): safe on block, ends in an overhead. 2, 1 cancels into Net or Sticky Bomb in his big combos. Starts combos
  • Kombat Boots (3, 3, 4): 3, 3 is safe on block (10 frames); mostly used inside juggles.
  • Power Plant (1, 2, 3): cancels into Buzzsaw.
  • Cyber Beatdown (Back+1, 2, 1, 2): press rapidly; an unblockable command throw that leaves them staggered.
  • Blast Overhead (Back+2): launcher, slow (30 frames). Good after Down+1 pokes make the opponent crouch-block. Starts combos

Combos

Specials used: Bomb close (Back, Back, 4), medium (Forward, Forward, 4), far (Back, Back, Forward, 4); Net (Back, Back, 3); Ragdoll (Down, Forward, 3, then throw); Reverse Kick (Down, Forward, 3); Buzzsaw (Back, Forward, 2). Enhanced: Sticky Bomb (EX Bomb).

  • Fast string: 1, 2, 1 xx Ragdoll (~23%)
  • Launcher: Back+2, dash, 1, 2, 1 xx Medium Bomb (~20%)
  • Jump-in, Net: jump-in Punch, 2, 1 xx Net, jump-in Punch, 1, 2, 3 xx Reverse Kick (~32%)
  • Jump-in, 1 bar: jump-in Punch, 2, 1 xx Sticky Bomb (EX close Bomb), Medium Bomb, Uppercut, Uppercut (~45%, 1 bar)
  • Big 1 bar: 2, 1 xx Sticky Bomb, Medium Bomb, Uppercut, Back+2, dash, 1, 2, 1 xx Ragdoll (~57%, 1 bar)
  • Net and bombs: jump-in Punch, 2, 1 xx Net, dash back, jump-in Punch, 2, 1 xx Sticky Bomb, Medium Bomb, Uppercut, Back+2, dash, 3 xx Ragdoll (~53%, 1 bar)
  • Jump-in, X-Ray: jump-in Punch, 2, 1 xx Net, jump-in Punch, 2, 1 xx X-Ray (~43%, 3 bars)
  • Bomb trap, X-Ray: Medium Bomb, Close Bomb, Net, Back+2, dash, 1 xx X-Ray (~41%, 3 bars)

How to play

Cyrax is a trap / zoner built on his Bombs (close, medium, far – unblockable) and the Net. Bombs and Net set up extended juggles and resets, and Back+1, 2, 1, 2 is an unblockable command throw. Lay bombs to control space, catch with the Net, then convert into long bomb-juggle combos. His routes are meter- and timing-heavy, and his Teleport is very unsafe outside combos.

Sources: MortalKombatOnline "Kombo Workshop" combo list; GameFAQs community Mortal Kombat Character Guide (2011); swiftninja's Midscreen Combo FAQ (GameFAQs).

Sektor

Useful strings

  • Demolition (2, 1, 4): 2, 1 cancels into Teleport Uppercut or Flame Burner. Starts combos
  • System Overload (1, 2, Back+1): Back+1 launches the opponent far for a long juggle. Back+1 on its own is safe on block and a good anti-air. Starts combos
  • Run Down (Back+3, 4): Back+3 hits low; cancel 4 into Teleport Uppercut. Starts combos
  • Fatal Error (Forward+4, 4): cancels into Teleport Uppercut; also used mid-juggle. Starts combos
  • Drive Power (Back+2, 1): Back+2 cancels into Teleport Uppercut. Starts combos
  • Fusion Force (1, 2, 4): avoid; the last hit tends to miss. Cancel 1, 2 into Flame Burner instead.

Combos

Specials used: Teleport Uppercut (Down, Forward, 4), Flame Burner (Back, Forward, 2), Straight Missile (Back, Forward, 1). Enhanced: Metal Uppercut (EX Teleport Uppercut).

  • Fast string: 2, 1 xx Teleport Uppercut, 2, 1 xx Flame Burner (~24%)
  • Low: Back+3, 4 xx Teleport Uppercut, 1, 2 xx Flame Burner (~25%)
  • Launcher: 1, 2, Back+1, Forward+4, 4 xx Teleport Uppercut, Back+2 xx Flame Burner (~36%)
  • Jump-in, 1 bar: jump-in Punch, 1, 2, Back+1, Forward+4, 4 xx Metal Uppercut (EX Teleport Uppercut), 4, Straight Missile (~42%, 1 bar; ~38% without meter)
  • Fast string, 1 bar: jump-in Punch, 2, 1 xx Teleport Uppercut, Jump Kick, Metal Uppercut (EX Teleport Uppercut), 4 xx Flame Burner (~39%, 1 bar)
  • Jump-in, X-Ray: jump-in Punch, 1, 2, Back+1, Forward+4, 4 xx Teleport Uppercut, 1, 2 xx X-Ray (~49%, 3 bars)
  • Back+2, X-Ray: Back+2 xx Teleport Uppercut, Forward+4 xx X-Ray (~43%, 3 bars)

How to play

Sektor is a zoner with fast Straight and Up Missiles and a quick Teleport Uppercut that doubles as a launcher. Zone with missiles, punish with Teleport Uppercut, and enhance it to extend juggles. Many of his strings are unsafe on block, so cancel safe hits into Teleport Uppercut and keep Back+1 for anti-air. Flame Burner ends combos and leaves the opponent standing.

Sources: MortalKombatOnline "Kombo Workshop" combo list; GameFAQs community Mortal Kombat Character Guide (2011); swiftninja's Midscreen Combo FAQ (GameFAQs).

Smoke

Useful strings

  • Combustion (3, Down+1, 2): his main juggle string and hit-confirm into Smoke Cloud. Starts combos
  • Rekindle (2, 1, 4): his fastest string; all high, knockdown. 2, 1 cancels into Smoke Cloud. Starts combos
  • Smokin (Back+2, 3): overhead starter; Back+2, 3 cancels into Smoke Cloud. Starts combos
  • On Fire (3, 2): filler inside juggles and corner loops.
  • Smoke has no low strings; his low option is the sweep. His mix-up comes from Smoke Towards and Smoke Away (teleports through the opponent) plus the Back+2 overhead.

Combos

Specials used: Smoke Cloud (Down, Back, 2), Teleport (Down, Back, 4; also in the air), Air Throw (throw while airborne). Enhanced: Smoke Bomb (EX Smoke Cloud), Smokeport (EX Teleport).

  • Fast string: 2, 1 xx Smoke Cloud, Jump Kick xx Teleport (~23%; ~26% with Smokeport)
  • Main string: 3, Down+1, 2, dash, Back+2, 3 xx Smoke Cloud, Jump Kick xx Air Throw (or Teleport) (~33%; ~42% with X-Ray after the Smoke Cloud)
  • Overhead: Back+2, 3 xx Smoke Cloud, step forward, 3, 2, dash, 3, Down+1, 2, Jump Kick xx Air Throw (~36%)
  • Jump-in: jump-in Punch, 3, Down+1, 2, dash, 3, 2 xx Smoke Cloud, dash, 3, Down+1, 2, Air Throw (~41%)
  • 1 bar: 3, Down+1, 2 xx Smoke Bomb (EX Smoke Cloud), 3, Down+1, 2, Jump Kick, Teleport (~36%, 1 bar; ~39% with Smokeport, 2 bars)
  • Reset loop: 3, Down+1, 2, Jump Kick xx Air Throw, OTG Smoke Cloud, dash, 3, Down+1, 2, Jump Kick xx Air Throw, OTG Smoke Cloud (~46%; the Smoke Cloud after an Air Throw can be blocked, so this is a reset, not a true combo)
  • X-Ray: 3, Down+1, 2 xx X-Ray (~40%, 3 bars)
  • Jump-in, X-Ray: jump-in Punch, 3, Down+1, 2 xx Smoke Cloud, 2, 1 xx X-Ray (~45%, 3 bars)
  • Corner: 2, 1, 4, Smoke Cloud, 3, 2, 3, 2, 3, 2, 2, 1, 4 (~41%, corner)

How to play

Smoke is a hit-confirm / mix-up character. Smoke Cloud (the enhanced version is Smoke Bomb) starts juggles and pulls opponents in, Shake counters projectiles (the enhanced Vibration also counters non-low normals), and Smoke Towards / Smoke Away pass through the opponent for cross-ups. After an Air Throw, throw a Smoke Cloud: the opponent can only block it. Condition them with 2, 1, 4 after Smoke Towards, then switch to the Back+2 overhead or a throw.

Sources: MortalKombatOnline "Kombo Workshop" combo list; GameFAQs community Mortal Kombat Character Guide (2011); swiftninja's Midscreen Combo FAQ (GameFAQs).

Reptile

Useful strings

  • Carnivore Bash (1, 2, 2, 1): 1, 2, 2 cancels into a Force Ball to start his juggle. Starts combos
  • Amphibian Charge (3, 2, 1): his juggle string; 3, 2 goes between Force Balls and 3, 2, 1 cancels into Slide or X-Ray. Starts combos
  • Hybrid Crush (2, Forward+3, 1+2): a meterless juggle ender.
  • Mix-ups: the Acid Hand special is an overhead and Slide hits low. Down+4 can be cancelled into Acid Hand for a low-into-overhead mix-up.

Combos

Specials used: Slow Force Ball (Back, Back, 1), Fast Force Ball (Back, Back, 3), Slide (Back, Forward, 4), Elbow Dash (Back, Forward, 2), Acid Hand (Down, Back, 2), Acid Spit (Down, Forward, 1). Enhanced: Reptilian Elbow (EX Elbow Dash), Acid Stream (EX Acid Spit). Each Force Ball type can only be used once per juggle.

  • Fast string: 1, 2, 2 xx Fast Force Ball, 3, 2 xx Slow Force Ball, 3, 2, 1 xx Slide (~34%; ~44% ending in X-Ray)
  • Jump-in: jump-in Punch, 1, 2, 2 xx Slow Force Ball, 3, 2 xx Fast Force Ball, 3, 2, 1 xx X-Ray (~46%, 3 bars)
  • From range: Fast Force Ball, Elbow Dash (it whiffs to close distance), NJP, 3, 2 xx Slow Force Ball, 3, 2, 1 xx Slide (~31%)
  • 3, 2, 1 starter: 3, 2, 1 xx Elbow Dash (whiffs), NJP, 3, 2 xx Slow Force Ball, Fast Force Ball, 3, 2, 1 xx Slide (~37%)
  • Short, 1 bar: 3, 2 xx Reptilian Elbow (EX Elbow Dash), Slide (~29%, 1 bar)
  • Keep them standing: end any combo above with 3, 2 xx Acid Stream (EX Acid Spit) instead of 3, 2, 1 xx Slide (1–2% less damage, 1 bar; prevents a wake-up attack)
  • Corner: 3, 2, 1 xx Acid Hand, 3, 2, 1 xx Fast Force Ball, 3, 2, 1 xx Acid Spit (~39%, corner)

How to play

Reptile is a juggle / rushdown character who can also zone. A Force Ball that hits gives a full combo from almost anywhere, Acid Spit is a very fast projectile, Elbow Dash is the fastest move in the game, and invisibility hides which attack is coming. Fire a Slow Force Ball and Elbow Dash in behind it: if they block, throw them. Slide hits low; Acid Hand is an overhead.

Sources: MortalKombatOnline "Kombo Workshop" combo list; GameFAQs community Mortal Kombat Character Guide (2011).

Kano

Useful strings

  • Dragon's Tail (Back+1, 1, 2): his best combo starter, leading into his most damaging juggles. Starts combos
  • Getting Ahead (2, 1, 2): easier starter, and his main juggle string (2, 1, 2, dash, 2, 1, 2). Starts combos
  • Mean Machine (Forward+3, Back+2): Back+2 (Overhead Smash) is an overhead launcher, so this catches opponents who block crouching. Starts combos

How to play

Freddy is a zoner / trapper with the Glove Toss (fast start-up and recovery) and Freddy Fingers (slow, but stops slides), the Hell Spikes (very fast ground spikes that also punish dive kicks), the Dream Shift teleport, and the Sweet Dreams sleep trap, which works best after a hard knockdown. Zone with projectiles and spikes, escape pressure with Dream Shift, and convert Hell Blast into long juggles. Nightmare Stance is a low stance that can't block, but non-low projectiles pass over it.
